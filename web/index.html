            Who's that Pokémon? Guess today's Pokémon from its silhouette

            Every day a Pokémon from each region draws itself with spinning circles. It starts as a blob and gets sharper with every miss. Can you name it with just 2 circles?

            How to play

            1. Every day there's a new Pokémon in each generation, from Kanto (Gen I) to Paldea (Gen IX), plus one with all 1025 mixed. The same for everyone.
            2. Its silhouette draws itself with 2 spinning circles: barely a sketch.
            3. Type a name or skip. Every miss adds circles: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 → 64.
            4. You have 6 tries.
            5. Stuck? Ask for a clue (type, evolution stage, category, color, size or first letter). Only one per game, picked at random.
            6. Every Pokémon you guess is saved as caught in your Pokédex, and the ones that get away as seen. Try to complete it.
            7. Share your result without spoilers and keep your daily streak going.

            Frequently asked questions

            What do circles have to do with the drawing?

            Any closed outline can be written as a sum of rotations: a Fourier series. Each circle spins at its own speed, mounted on the previous one, and the tip of the last one traces the silhouette. Few circles give the rough shape; many give the details. Those chained circles are called epicycles.

            When is the new Pokémon released?

            Every day at midnight, Madrid time (CET). Everyone gets the same Pokémon that day.

            Which Pokémon can appear?

            All 1025, from Bulbasaur to Pecharunt. Pick a generation (Gen I is the default) or play them all mixed: each mode has its own Pokémon of the day and its own streak.

            Is it free? Do I need an account?

            It's free and there's no sign-up. Your progress, streak and statistics are saved only in your browser.

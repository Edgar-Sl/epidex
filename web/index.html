          Who's that Pokémon? Guess today's Pokémon from its silhouette

          Every day a Gen 1 Pokémon draws itself with spinning circles. It starts as a blob and gets sharper with every miss. Can you name it with just 2 circles?

          How to play

          1. Every day there's a new Pokémon from the first generation (Kanto, #001–#151), the same for everyone.
          2. Its silhouette draws itself with 2 spinning circles: barely a sketch.
          3. Type a name or skip. Every miss adds circles: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 → 64.
          4. You have 6 tries. Then answer two bonus questions: its type and its evolution family.
          5. Share your result without spoilers and keep your daily streak going.

          Frequently asked questions

          What do circles have to do with the drawing?

          Any closed outline can be written as a sum of rotations: a Fourier series. Each circle spins at its own speed, mounted on the previous one, and the tip of the last one traces the silhouette. Few circles give the rough shape; many give the details. Those chained circles are called epicycles.

          When is the new Pokémon released?

          Every day at midnight, Madrid time (CET). Everyone gets the same Pokémon that day.

          Which Pokémon can appear?

          The 151 Pokémon of the first generation, from Bulbasaur to Mew.

          Is it free? Do I need an account?

          It's free and there's no sign-up. Your progress, streak and statistics are saved only in your browser.
